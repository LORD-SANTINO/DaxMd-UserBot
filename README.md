## ✨ Features

- 🧠 **AI Auto‑Reply** – GPT‑4 / DeepSeek powered, with custom personality, tone, and language
- 📢 **Broadcast Hub** – Send one message to all your private chats, groups, and channels at once
- 👥 **Group Management** – Kick, ban, mute, promote, pin, purge, and more
- 📅 **Scheduled Messages** – Queue messages for any date and time
- 🎥 **Social Media Downloader** – Download videos from YouTube, TikTok, Facebook, and Instagram
- 🛡️ **Anti‑Delete / Anti‑Link** – Capture deleted messages and auto‑remove links
- 💬 **Mini App** – Full control panel inside Telegram (`/myai` or direct button)
- 🏷️ **My Chats** – View all your groups & channels with role tags, search, sort, and bulk leave
- 📡 **Command Playground** – Toggle bot state live and search 100+ commands
- 💰 **Affiliate Program** – Invite friends and earn Telegram Stars
- 📖 **Auto‑Read** – Mark messages as read in private chats, groups, or channels
- 🔒 **Business Secretary** – Handle Telegram Business profile messages automatically
- ⏰ **Always Online** – Stay online 24/7
- 🔄 **Auto‑React** – React to messages with custom emojis
- … and **100+ commands**!


## 🚀 Quick Start

1. Open [@DAXMDrobot](https://t.me/DAXMDrobot) and send `/start`
2. Enter your phone number (with country code)
3. Verify with the code (add spaces between digits!)
4. Done! Your userbot is online.

> 💡 Use `/myai` to open the Mini App for advanced settings, or open it directly: [t.me/DAXMDrobot/core](https://t.me/DAXMDrobot/core)

## ⚙️ How It Works

DAX-MD is a **userbot** – it logs into your personal Telegram account using Telethon and acts as you. It runs 24/7 on cloud servers, so your phone doesn't need to stay online. Your session is encrypted and stored securely in MongoDB.

All automation features can be toggled from the Mini App or via commands in any chat.


## 🔒 Privacy & Security

- Your **session string is encrypted** and stored securely
- **No passwords are stored**
- Use `/stop` to **immediately revoke access** and delete your session
- The bot only responds to commands from the account owner (in private mode)
